About & Guide

How to read and use this dashboard

Purpose

A single view of visitor bookings, activities, feedback, ratings and complaints for Red Sea Global destinations – built from the Visitor Experience Survey (n = 511; 261 Arabic · 250 English).

How to use

  • Use the four filters (Destination, Visitor type, Region, Month) – every KPI and chart recalculates instantly.
  • Click any KPI card to open a drill-down with its monthly trend and destination breakdown.
  • Click a bar, donut slice or month point to cross-filter; click it again to remove the filter.
  • Survey Data – search, sort, page and export the filtered responses to CSV.
  • Alerts List – assign owners, start work and close tickets; the dashboard updates live and your changes are saved in this browser.
  • Analysis Tables – item statistics, reliability, correlations, group means and SPSS tests.

Definitions

  • Avg satisfaction – mean of Q26 (1–5). Positive rate – share of Q26 answers of 4 or 5.
  • NPS (5-point proxy) – % promoters minus % detractors, grouped from Q27.
  • Open alerts – alerts not yet closed, honoring the active filters. SLA = 24 h.
  • Overall – mean of the four dimensions: Experience, Feedback, Smart platform, Outcome.

Data note

Satisfaction, recommendation, region and item scores are real survey responses. Destination, visitor type, month and alert records are illustrative prototype data added for demonstration.
